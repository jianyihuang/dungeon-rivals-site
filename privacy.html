Dungeon Rivals

Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Dungeon Rivals is a personal tool operated by the channel owner to publish original animated fantasy battle videos to YouTube. It is listed as Youtube Upload App in Google’s OAuth settings. There is no public registration. This policy describes the current YouTube upload connection and this informational website.

Information accessed and used

The owner authorizes the app through Google. The current connection requests youtube.upload permission to upload videos to the selected YouTube channel.

  • Publishing content: the owner's video files, titles, descriptions, categories, visibility settings, audience settings, and synthetic-content declarations are sent to YouTube for publication.
  • Authorization: OAuth credentials allow the workflow to obtain access tokens and upload without requiring the owner to sign in for every run. The app does not receive the Google account password.
  • Upload results: the workflow records the video ID, video link, and visibility reported by YouTube, together with publishing timestamps and workflow status.

These records are used to publish the owner's content, troubleshoot uploads, and prevent duplicate posts. The current upload connection does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or YouTube Analytics.

Storage and retention

OAuth refresh tokens and client credentials are stored in owner-controlled local credential files and encrypted GitHub repository secrets. Authorized workflow jobs use those credentials when communicating with Google. Current credentials are kept out of source control and generated-media artifacts.

Publishing records and upload receipts are stored locally and in the owner's private GitHub repository and workflow records. Generated videos and upload-result artifacts in GitHub Actions are configured for 90-day retention. Local files remain until the owner deletes them. Publishing history is kept to prevent duplicate posts and remains until the owner deletes it.

Services and sharing

Google and YouTube process authorization requests, uploaded videos, and publishing information. GitHub provides the automation, secret storage, and private operational records used by the owner. Published videos and their metadata are visible according to the visibility settings applied by YouTube.

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train AI models. Its use of Google user data is limited to the publishing and operational purposes described in this policy.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. This app is independently operated and is not a Google or YouTube product.

Remove access or request deletion

The owner can revoke authorization in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete existing local files, publishing records, or videos already uploaded to YouTube.

To request deletion of stored authorization or account-related records, contact memeowz420@gmail.com. The owner can remove the local credential files and GitHub secrets and address retained publishing records. Uploaded videos can be managed or deleted separately in YouTube Studio.

This website

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process standard request information to deliver and protect the site. If you contact the support email, your message and email address are used to respond to your request.

Updates and contact

Changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and its effective date. Any new Google permissions require a separate authorization request.

Questions about Dungeon Rivals or this policy: memeowz420@gmail.com.
