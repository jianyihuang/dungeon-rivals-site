Dungeon Rivals

A personal publisher for original fantasy battles. Dungeon Rivals creates animated fantasy battle videos and uploads them to the owner's authorized YouTube channel, with the video's title, description, and publishing settings.

The publisher is listed as Youtube Upload App in Google’s OAuth settings.

Operated by the channel owner · No public registration

01 / CREATE

Make an original video

The owner runs a workflow that renders a short animated battle and prepares its publishing information.

02 / AUTHORIZE

Connect through Google

The owner grants YouTube upload access through Google's authorization screen. The app does not receive the Google account password.

03 / PUBLISH

Upload and keep a record

The workflow uploads the video and saves its upload result to support troubleshooting and prevent duplicate posts.

Access for a specific purpose

The current Google connection requests YouTube upload permission. It is used for the owner's publishing workflow; this site does not offer Google sign-in or a publishing service for other users.

Read the privacy policy for details about authorization, stored records, and removing access. For support, contact memeowz420@gmail.com.
